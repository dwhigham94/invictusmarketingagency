SMS Messaging Consent & Opt-In Disclosure

By providing your phone number and opting in to receive text messages from Invictus Marketing Group LLC, you consent to receive SMS communications related to:

  • Health insurance enrollment information and plan options
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Open Enrollment period notifications
  • Follow-up regarding your insurance inquiry

Message frequency: Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 10 messages per month depending on your enrollment status and inquiry.

Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier's standard messaging and data rates apply to any messages you send or receive.

Opt-out: You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message and will no longer receive SMS from us unless you re-subscribe.

Help: Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at contact@invictusmarketingagency.com.

Privacy: Your phone number and personal information will never be sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. We use your information solely to assist you with your insurance needs. View our Privacy Policy for full details.

By opting in, you acknowledge that you are the owner or authorized user of the phone number provided and that you agree to receive the messages described above. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
