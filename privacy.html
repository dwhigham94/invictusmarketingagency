Privacy Policy

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers licensed insurance agents and prospective agents who interact with BookdAI — our appointment-setting platform for health and life insurance professionals — including those who sign up, request a demo, or opt in to receive messages from us.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, business email, mobile phone number, and details about your agency or practice that you submit through our sign-up forms, demo requests, or onboarding process.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide, set up, and support your BookdAI account and services
  • Contact you via SMS about onboarding, account and service updates, demo scheduling, and appointment confirmations
  • With separate consent, send marketing messages about new features, pricing offers, and platform updates
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. SMS messaging & consent

We collect SMS consent through two separate, unchecked checkboxes on our opt-in form — one for transactional/customer-care messages and one for marketing messages. You may consent to either, both, or neither, and consent is never a condition of purchasing or using BookdAI.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP or contact us at support@bookdai.com.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent data with any third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Your opt-in is used only to deliver the messages you selected from BookdAI.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with service providers who help us operate the platform and deliver messages (for example, our SMS provider and CRM), and with legal or regulatory authorities when required by law. These providers are bound to use your information only to provide services to us.

6. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Opt-out requests are honored promptly and your number is suppressed from future outreach.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting support@bookdai.com.

8. Cookies & analytics

Our website may use basic analytics to understand traffic and usage. We do not use cookies for advertising tracking.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for licensed insurance professionals and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our services after notice of changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
